FAQ

Questions owners ask first

What is MCP?

Model Context Protocol is an open standard that lets AI assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT connect to other tools. Ads MCP is the connector that lets your AI read your ad accounts and, with your confirmation, make changes, within the permissions you grant.

Do I need to be technical?

No. If you can paste a link into your AI’s settings and sign in to your ad accounts, you can set it up. The guide shows every screen.

Which ad platforms does it work with?

Meta (Facebook and Instagram), TikTok, Google Ads and OpenAI Ads. Ads AI adds research from the Meta Ad Library, TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and Google Maps reviews.

Can it change my campaigns without me?

No. Every change shows a preview and waits for you to confirm it. New campaigns, ads and copies are always created paused, and every change is logged with a reason.

I work with an agency. Is this for me?

Yes. Owners use it to see the real numbers behind their agency’s reports and to get answers without waiting for the next call.

How much does it cost?

Plans depend on how many ad accounts you manage and whether you choose Ads MCP or Ads AI. Leave your details and we’ll send current pricing for your region with the guide.

How is my data handled?

Ads MCP works through the access you grant when you sign in, and you can disconnect it anytime from your AI’s connector settings. Our Privacy Policy explains what we collect and how to ask us to access or delete your data.
